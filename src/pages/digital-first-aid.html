Digital First Aid

Free, practical help with digital basics for small businesses and community projects. No sale attached.

How it works

Why it exists

Why it exists.

Some businesses and projects serve a lot of people and need a hand with their digital basics. Digital First Aid is how we give some of our time to them.

It isn't tied to one city. Most sessions can happen over a video call.

Who it's for

Businesses and projects that help others.

Humanitarian projects.

Community and volunteer projects.

Small businesses whose success benefits the people around them.

What a session can include

The basics, done properly.

  1. Setting up or fixing a Google Business Profile.
  2. Setting up a WhatsApp Business catalogue.
  3. Shared files and accounts for a team or volunteers.
  4. A checklist for accepting card payments.
  5. Clear advice on what to fix next.

We're still shaping the programme. Tell us what you need and we'll say plainly whether we can help.
