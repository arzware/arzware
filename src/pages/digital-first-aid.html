Digital First Aid

Quick, practical digital help for small businesses and community projects that need it, from people who would rather fix it than sell it.

Why it exists

Community before money.

Money isn't our main goal. Some businesses and projects help a lot of people and need a hand with their digital basics. Digital First Aid is how we give that help.

It isn't tied to one neighbourhood or city.

Who it's for

Businesses and projects that help others.

Humanitarian projects.

Community help projects.

Small businesses whose success benefits the people around them.

What a session can include

The basics, done properly.

  1. Setting up or fixing a Google Business Profile.
  2. Setting up a WhatsApp Business catalogue.
  3. A checklist for accepting card payments.
  4. Honest advice on what to fix next.

We're still shaping the programme. Tell us what you need and we'll say honestly whether we can help.
