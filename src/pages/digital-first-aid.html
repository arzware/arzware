Digital First Aid

Our community programme: practical digital help for small businesses and community projects that need it most. It isn't a sales programme.

Why we do it

Community before money.

Money isn't our main goal. We want to help businesses that, in turn, help other people, and that sometimes means doing the work for free.

Digital First Aid is where that happens. We look for small, practical fixes that make a real difference quickly: being findable online, taking orders and messages in one place, and accepting card payments.

Who it's for

Small businesses and community projects.

Small businesses

Shops, cafés and family businesses that are struggling and need a practical digital fix.

Community projects

Groups and initiatives whose work helps the people around them.

Humanitarian organisations

Teams doing important work with limited budgets and tools.
