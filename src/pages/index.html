Questions

What people usually ask first.

Something else? Ask us directly.

How does the first call work?

It's free, and it's a business conversation. You tell us how your business works and what's bothering you. We ask questions, and we tell you honestly whether we can help.

What happens in a diagnosis?

We collect information about your business, research it online, check two or three competitors, and give you written findings with evidence and fixes in priority order. See the full method.

How do you price?

Every business is different, so we quote after a short conversation. You'll get a written scope and a fixed price before anything starts.

Do you work remotely with the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Most of our work happens over calls, WhatsApp and shared documents. We're in Beirut, within two hours of Riyadh and Dubai.

Which AI providers do you use, and where does my data go?

It depends on the job, and we name them before anything is connected. If your data needs to stay with you, we can run AI locally instead.

What if you can't do what I need?

We'll say so, and connect you with a partner we trust. If we refer you, we'll tell you who they are.
