Our thesis

Understanding should come before AI.

AI is coming to every business. Too often it arrives the wrong way: tools bought before anyone understood the problem, data handed over without knowing where it goes, automation that pushes people out instead of helping them.

For Arzware, that means working in the opposite order. Understand the business first. Use AI where it truly helps. Keep people in charge. And make sure the benefit reaches past the business, to its customers, its team and its community.

Honest answers

What people ask us, answered plainly.

If your question isn't here, ask us. We'll give you a straight answer.

Who is Arzware?

A business development and digital solutions company. We research how a business works, show what's holding it back, and then build the fix or introduce a partner who can. We were founded by computer science graduates with backgrounds in software engineering, cybersecurity, AI automation and business design.

Where are you based, and how do you work with businesses in the Gulf?

Our office is in Hamra, Beirut. Most of our work with businesses in the UAE and Saudi Arabia happens over video calls, WhatsApp and shared documents. Beirut is within two hours of Riyadh and Dubai, so our working day overlaps with yours.

Do you have case studies?

Not published yet. We're a young company, and we only publish client work with the client's permission. Until then, we show you how we work: our method, a sample report and our research. On a call, we'll walk you through how we would approach your business.

How much does it cost?

We don't publish prices, because every business needs something different. After a short conversation you get a written scope and a fixed price before any work starts. No surprises after that.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It's a business conversation, not a sales pitch. You tell us how the business works and what's bothering you. We tell you honestly whether we can help.

Do you do everything yourselves?

No. We build what we're good at. When something isn't, we introduce you to a partner we trust, and we tell you who they are before anything is agreed.

Which AI providers touch my data?

We name every provider before anything is connected, and explain what each one can see. If your data needs to stay with you, we can run AI locally instead. Accounts are set up in your name wherever possible.

Who won't you work with?

Businesses whose main purpose is taking people's money unfairly, and any project that requires breaking the law.
