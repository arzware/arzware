Questions

Straight answers.

If your question isn't here, ask us. We'd rather answer it than have you guess.

What exactly does Arzware do?

We look at how a business works, find what's holding it back, and fix it. The fixes are usually practical tools: a website, WhatsApp booking or ordering, a CRM, Google Workspace and AI, a POS setup, and monthly care. When something is outside what we do, we connect you with a partner we trust.

How much does it cost?

We don't publish prices. Every business is different, and we'd rather quote after we understand yours. You'll get a quote in writing before any work starts.

Is the first meeting really free?

Yes. It's a business conversation: you tell us how your business works, we ask questions, and we tell you honestly whether we can help.

Can I see work you've done for other clients?

Not on this website yet. We only publish client work when the client agrees, and we'll add case studies here as they're ready. Until then, you can see exactly how we work, and a sample of what a diagnosis looks like, on How we work.

You're in Beirut. Can you work with my business in Dubai or Riyadh?

Yes. The UAE and Saudi Arabia are our main focus. Most of the work happens over calls, video, WhatsApp and shared documents, and Beirut is within two hours of both.

What if you can't do what I need?

We'll say so. If it's outside what we do, we'll connect you with a partner we trust and tell you who they are.

What happens to my data, and which AI tools do you use?

We tell you exactly which AI providers we connect before anything is connected. If your data needs to stay with you, we can run AI locally instead.

Is there anyone you won't work with?

Yes. We don't work with businesses whose main purpose is taking people's money unfairly, and we don't take on anything that requires breaking the law.
