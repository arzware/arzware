How we work

We don't recommend anything we haven't researched.

A business might call us asking for a website. Before we build anything, we look at the whole business and check what it actually needs. These are the five steps, always in this order.

The method

Five steps.

  1. 01

    Collect

    We learn how your business works: what you sell, how you take orders, how customers reach you, and which tools you use.

    What happensA conversation with you.

  2. 02

    Research

    We study your business online: your website, social media, Google listing and reviews.

    What we gatherNotes and screenshots.

  3. 03

    Check competitors

    We find nearby competitors and compare their online presence and tools with yours, on the same points.

    What you seeHow you compare, side by side.

  4. 04

    Diagnose

    We name the problems slowing the business down, including the ones you didn't mention.

    What you getFindings, each with its evidence.

  5. 05

    Offer help

    We recommend what to fix, in what order. We build what we can, and refer a trusted partner for anything outside what we do.

    What you decideWhat happens next. The findings are yours either way.

Build or connect

We build what we're good at. For the rest, we bring in someone we trust.

We'd like to be the company a business can call about anything. When a need is outside what we do, we refer a trusted partner from our network, a company or a freelancer, and tell you who they are.

The aim is that everyone involved benefits: you get the right help, the partner gets good work, and we earn your trust for the next problem.

After launch

We stay around.

Tools need looking after once they're live. Our monthly care plans cover updates, fixes and small changes, with a short note on what's worth improving next.
