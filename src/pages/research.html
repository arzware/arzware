How we research

Our rules for every note.

  • Official sources first: regulators, ministries and the companies that make the tools.
  • Every note lists its sources and the date it was last checked.
  • Plain words, written for business owners, not specialists.
  • Research, not legal, tax or financial advice. For decisions, check with a qualified professional.
