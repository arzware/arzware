Approach

Research first. Then everything else.

A client might call us asking for a website. Before we build anything, we look at the whole business and check what it actually needs. Here is exactly how that works.

The five steps

How a diagnosis works.

  1. 01

    Collect

    We do

    Gather what you sell, how you take orders, how customers reach you and which tools you use.

    You do

    Talk to us on a free call and share what you're comfortable sharing.

  2. 02

    Research

    We do

    Study your business online: website, social media, Google listing and reviews.

    You do

    Nothing. This part is on us.

  3. 03

    Check competitors

    We do

    Find two or three nearby competitors and compare their presence and tools with yours.

    You get

    Context: where you're ahead, and where they are.

  4. 04

    Diagnose

    We do

    Identify what's slowing the business down, including things you didn't mention.

    You get

    Written findings, each with evidence.

  5. 05

    Offer help

    We do

    Recommend what to fix in what order, what we can do, and who we'd refer for the rest.

    You do

    Decide. The report is yours either way.

How we use AI

AI is in everything we do. So are people.

We use AI at every step: to research a business and its market, to find patterns in how customers behave, and inside the tools we build. We use it carefully, and we tell you exactly how.

  1. We name every AI provider before anything is connected, and explain what each one can see.
  2. If your data needs to stay with you, we can run AI locally instead.
  3. AI gets one clear job at a time, such as bookings, order questions or drafting replies.
  4. A person can always step in, and a person checks what matters before it goes out.
  5. We use AI to help people, never to mislead them.

Build or connect

We build what we can. We connect you for the rest.

When a fix is something we do well, we offer to build it, with a written scope and a fixed price. When it isn't, we refer a partner from our network: a company or an individual we trust.

Everyone should win: you get the right person for the job, the partner gets good work, and we become the company you can call about anything.

If we refer you to a partner, we'll tell you who they are before anything is agreed.

After launch

We don't disappear.

Tools need looking after. Our monthly care plans cover updates, fixes, security and small changes, and on larger plans a regular check-up on how things are going.

Principles

How we behave.

Honest diagnosis

We tell you what you really need, with proof, even when it isn't what you asked for. Sometimes that means telling you that you don't need us.

Everyone wins

Every deal should benefit everyone involved: you, any partner we bring in, and us.

Community before money

Money isn't the main goal. Sometimes we do work for free, through Digital First Aid, and we're comfortable with that.

Always within the law

We never take part in anything illegal, and we won't build anything that requires breaking the law.

Who we won't work with

Some work we turn down.

  • Businesses whose main purpose is taking people's money unfairly.
  • Any project that requires breaking the law.
