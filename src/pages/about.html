About

A technology company that studies the business first.

Arzware sets up websites, WhatsApp assistants, CRM, POS and AI tools for growing businesses in Lebanon, the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Every project starts with understanding how the business works.

The name

Arz and ware.

Arz is the cedar, the tree of Lebanon. Ware, as in software. Cedars grow slowly and last for centuries. We want what we set up to last too.

Why we exist

Why we started.

Small and medium businesses are offered more tools than ever, and little help choosing between them. We started Arzware to do the research first and set up only what a business needs.

We don't just make websites. A business calls us about one problem, and we look at the whole picture first. Sometimes the answer is a website. Often it's something else.

How we're built

Engineers who listen to the business first.

Arzware's founders studied computer science and work across software engineering, cybersecurity, AI automation and business design.

The people who research your business are the people who set up the solution. For work outside what we do well, we bring in a partner and name them first.
