About

The company you can call about anything in your business.

Arzware is an AI and business development company. We research how a business works, then build the intelligent tools that help it, and the people around it, grow.

The name

Arz and ware.

Arz is the cedar, the tree of Lebanon. Ware is technology. Together they stand for what we try to bring to every business: something that grows steadily, built with modern tools.

Why we exist

To help growing businesses work with modern tools.

We help small and medium businesses grow their presence and speed up their work, while building a network where clients, partners and freelancers all benefit.

We don't just make websites. A business calls us about one problem, and we look at the whole picture first. Sometimes the answer is a website. Often it's something else.

How we're built

Engineers who listen to the business first.

Arzware was founded by computer science graduates with backgrounds in software engineering, cybersecurity, AI automation and business design.

Everyone helps across every part of the work. For anything outside what we do well, we bring in partners from our network.

Where we are

Hamra, Beirut.

We work with businesses in Lebanon, the UAE and Saudi Arabia, mostly over video calls, WhatsApp and shared documents.

Meetings are by appointment.
