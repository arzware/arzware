About

We are Arzware.

A business development and digital solutions company, based in Hamra, Beirut. We work with growing businesses in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, and with businesses in Lebanon.

The name

Arz and ware.

Arz is the Arabic word for cedar. Ware stands for technology, as in software and hardware.

The name joins nature and technology, which we like combining. Technology today is huge, busy and often overwhelming. We want to find a calm, clear way through it for the businesses we work with.

Who we are

Founded by computer science graduates.

With backgrounds in software engineering, cybersecurity, AI automation and business design, and the habit of helping each other across all of them.

  1. 01We diagnose a business before we recommend anything, and we show the evidence.
  2. 02We build what we're good at, and connect you with a partner we trust for the rest.
  3. 03We want to be helpful, understanding and bright-minded. Never selfish, busy or greedy.
  4. 04We're building a network where clients, partners and freelancers all benefit.

Why we exist

To help growing businesses work with modern tools.

We help small and medium businesses grow their digital presence and give them tools that make their work faster and more modern.

Over time we want to grow a network of partner companies and freelancers, so that when a business calls us, we can either solve the problem or connect it with someone who can.

Values

What we hold ourselves to.

Honest diagnosis

We tell clients what they really need, with proof, even when it isn't what they asked for.

Everyone wins

Every deal should benefit everyone involved: the client, the partner and us.

Community before money

We want to help businesses that help other people, and sometimes that means working for free.

Always within the law

We never take part in anything outside the law.

Who we won't work with. Businesses whose main purpose is taking people's money unfairly, and any project that requires breaking the law. Who we especially want to work with: humanitarian and community projects, and businesses whose success benefits the people around them.

Where we are

Hamra, Beirut.

Our main focus is the UAE, starting with Dubai, and Saudi Arabia. We also work with businesses in Lebanon. Meetings are by appointment, and most of our work happens over calls, video, WhatsApp and shared documents.
