Privacy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: what we collect, why, and who sees it

  • This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • The contact form doesn't store anything. It opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message.
  • When you book a call, cal.com handles the booking. When you message us, WhatsApp or email carries the message.
  • We use what you send us only to reply and to work with you. We don't sell it.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Who we are

Arzware, based in Hamra, Beirut, Lebanon. Contact: support@arzware.net. Our registered company details will be added here once registration is complete.

What this website collects

The website itself doesn't ask for personal data. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics, we'll use a privacy-friendly, cookieless tool and update this page.

When you contact us

  • Booking a call: cal.com collects the details you enter when booking.
  • WhatsApp: messages are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms.
  • Email: messages are handled by Google Workspace, which we use for email.

When you work with us

If we work together, we tell you in writing which tools and AI providers will process your data, and why, before anything is connected. Where possible, accounts are set up in your name.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and business information only as long as we need them to reply, deliver work or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, by writing to support@arzware.net. This page is written with Lebanon's Law No. 81 of 2018 on electronic transactions and personal data in mind.
