Industries

Different businesses get stuck in different places.

We work across many industries. These are the ones we focus on, and what we usually look at first. Every business is different, so your diagnosis decides what's true for you.

01

Cafés and restaurants

Bakeries, coffee shops and independent restaurants.

The café and restaurant package

What we look at

  • Your Google listing, menu and reviews
  • How orders and bookings come in
  • How you take payment and count sales

Problems we often see

  • A menu that only exists as a photo or PDF
  • Old opening hours and unanswered reviews
  • Orders lost in WhatsApp at peak hours
What we'd typically set up: a menu website and QR menu, a Google profile kept up to date, WhatsApp ordering and a POS that fits.

02

Food chains

Groups with several branches, kitchens or brands.

POS setup for several branches

What we look at

  • How each branch takes orders and reports sales
  • Menus and prices across branches and channels
  • Catering and corporate orders

Problems we often see

  • Each branch does things its own way
  • Prices drift between the menu, the website and delivery apps
  • Reports put together by hand every week
What we'd typically set up: one menu and ordering flow for every branch, a POS with shared reporting, and a CRM for larger orders.

03

Tech companies

Startups and small software teams selling to businesses.

CRM setup

What we look at

  • How leads arrive and who follows them up
  • Whether your website speaks to buyers
  • Which tools and AI services your team uses

Problems we often see

  • Leads tracked in spreadsheets and chats
  • A website written for engineers, not customers
  • AI tools in use without agreed rules
What we'd typically set up: a CRM with a clear pipeline, a website written for your customers, and Workspace and AI rules your team can follow.

04

Other growing businesses

Clinics, shops, salons and professional services.

The business diagnosis

What we look at

  • How customers book and how you confirm
  • Where questions arrive and who answers
  • Where your files and accounts live

Problems we often see

  • The same questions answered by hand all day
  • Bookings spread across calls, chats and paper
  • Work files on personal phones
What we'd typically set up: online booking with WhatsApp reminders, a simple CRM or shared inbox, and professional email with shared files.
