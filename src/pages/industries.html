Industries

Different businesses get stuck in different places.

We work across many industries. These are the ones we focus on first. What follows are common patterns, not statistics: your diagnosis tells us what's true for you.

01

Cafés and restaurants

Customers decide fast, usually on their phone. A missing menu or wrong opening hours can lose them before they arrive.

What we often look at

  • The menu online, and how easy it is to read on a phone
  • The Google listing: hours, photos, reviews
  • How orders and bookings arrive

What we'd typically set up

  • A menu website and QR menu
  • WhatsApp ordering or booking
  • A POS that fits
Café and restaurant package

02

Food chains

Each new branch adds a little more difference: menus, prices and reports start to drift apart.

What we often look at

  • Whether menus and prices match across branches and channels
  • How sales are reported
  • How catering and corporate orders are followed up

What we'd typically set up

  • One menu and ordering flow for every branch
  • A POS with shared reporting
  • A CRM for larger orders
POS setup

03

Tech companies

Strong products, but sales and internal tools often grow faster than anyone planned for.

What we often look at

  • How leads are tracked and followed up
  • Whether the website speaks to buyers or to engineers
  • How the team uses AI tools, and with what rules

What we'd typically set up

  • A CRM with a clear pipeline
  • A website written for customers
  • Google Workspace and AI rules the team can follow

04

Community and humanitarian organisations

We especially want to work with projects whose success helps other people.

Teams doing good work often run on tight budgets and borrowed tools. We help them set up practical systems, and some of this work is done through our community programme.

05

Other growing businesses

Clinics, shops, service firms and family businesses often share the same problems.

What we often look at

  • Bookings spread across calls, chats and paper
  • The same questions answered by hand all day
  • Files and accounts on personal phones

What we'd typically set up

  • Booking with WhatsApp reminders
  • A simple CRM or shared inbox
  • Professional email and shared files
